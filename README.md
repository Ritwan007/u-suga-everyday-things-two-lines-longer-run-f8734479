A two-line explanation of rain for a 10-year-old, built on a concrete,
surprising analogy — a bucket of water poured through a colander — with an
honest second line about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## The entry

> Rain is the sky pouring a bucket of water through a colander, so it comes down in thousands of little drops instead of one big splash.
> The bucket is the cloud, the colander's holes are what break the water into drops, but there is no bucket or colander up there, and the water was invisible vapour, not a stored puddle.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (319 characters, exactly
two lines, one sentence each) and self-checks the gate before writing.
